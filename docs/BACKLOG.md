# Ideas for later

These are in the app. Home still opens on the four counts, the week, and the task list. The rest lives on the person page, Review, Set aside, and Settings.

1. Understand dates typed in a sentence, such as “Friday”, and show the reading before it is saved.
2. Open the new-meeting sheet from anywhere on the Mac with a shortcut, even when the app is in the background.
3. A command menu that finds a person, a meeting, or a task, and can mark a task done.
4. Drag a task onto another day to change its due date.
5. A small menu-bar count of what is overdue and due today.
6. A reminder on this Mac on the due date, which you can turn off per task.
7. Save one meeting, or the whole board, as a text file or spreadsheet in a folder you choose.
8. Keep an automatic backup of the private database on this Mac.
9. Open a person or a meeting in a second window beside the home screen.
10. Repeating tasks.
11. A priority mark on a task.
12. A checklist inside one task.
13. Tags.
14. A “waiting on them” state that leaves your due list but stays on their page for the next meeting.
15. A next check-in date on a person, shown on the home screen next to tasks.
16. A repeating check-in rhythm that moves forward when you log a meeting.
17. When a check-in date passes with no meeting, ask whether to log one, snooze a week, or dismiss it.
18. A page that prepares the next conversation: open tasks, what you finished since last time, and the standing note.
19. The typical number of days a request takes, shown only after there is enough finished history.
20. A five-week calendar of how crowded each day is, with the count written on the day.
21. A side list of tasks that have no due date, which you can drag onto a day.
22. A bar from the day they asked to the due day, so you can see how long they have been waiting.
23. One filter for open tasks that have not changed in a long time.
24. Saved views you build yourself.
25. A morning step that asks you to pick three tasks for today and move the rest.
26. Snooze a due date by a day or a week.
27. Mark several tasks done at once.
28. A soft color for each person, used on the week strip.
29. Start typing anywhere to jump to a person or a task.
30. Meeting templates for the kinds of conversations you repeat.
31. A place for decisions that is separate from tasks.
32. Several people in one meeting.
33. A company or group that holds people.
34. A dark appearance.
35. A one-page brief you can read before the next meeting.
36. A weekly recap: what was asked, what was finished, and which days were heavy.
37. Undo and redo for every change, not only the meeting you just saved.
38. Attach a file that stays next to the database on this Mac.
39. Subtasks.
40. A history of what changed on a task.
41. A short list of keyboard shortcuts in the app.
42. Its own band for tasks that have been waiting a long time, even when the due date is still ahead.
43. An “in progress” state between open and done.
44. Sort a list so the tasks closest to done sit at the top.
45. Natural dates while typing a task, with a tap to turn a misread word back into plain text.
46. Keep the new-meeting sheet open after saving, so the next task is ready.
47. Show calendar events on the week strip as a quiet reference, without turning them into tasks.
48. A “this evening” group for tasks you want to leave until later today.
49. Bulk edit of due dates.
50. Print the home screen.
51. A count of how many times a task has been moved.
52. Hide finished meetings on a person’s page, with a way to show them again.
53. Pin one task to the top of the home screen.
54. Notes on a single task, separate from the meeting note.
55. Highlight the matching words in search results.
56. Remember the last person you logged, and offer them first in the new-meeting sheet.
57. A quiet prompt when a day on the week strip has more tasks than you usually take.
58. Export only one person’s open tasks.
59. An archive of set-aside tasks, separate from the meeting page.
60. Keyboard moving between the home groups: overdue, today, this week, later.
61. A larger type size setting.
62. Restore a removed person from the backup in item 8.
63. Show the median wait, not the average, so one old task does not distort the number.
64. A sparkline of how many tasks were open over the last twelve weeks.
65. A review that walks each person and asks whether their open tasks are still real.
66. Ask before closing the app when a meeting sheet still has unsaved typing.
